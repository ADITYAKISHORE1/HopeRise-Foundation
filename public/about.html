Our Story

Born From a Simple Belief:
Everyone Deserves a Chance

HopeRise Foundation was founded in 2012 by a group of passionate changemakers who believed that sustainable development starts at the community level. What began as a single education program in a rural Indian village has grown into a global organization operating in 50+ countries.

Today, we've impacted over 2 million lives through our integrated approach to education, healthcare, clean water, and economic empowerment. But we're just getting started.

Our Foundation

Mission, Vision & Values

🎯

Our Mission

To empower underprivileged communities through sustainable education, healthcare, and economic development programs that create lasting change.

🔭

Our Vision

A world where every person — regardless of where they are born — has access to the tools and opportunities needed to build a dignified, fulfilling life.

💎

Our Values

Transparency, community ownership, sustainability, dignity, and the unwavering belief that every human life has equal worth and potential.

Our Journey

Milestones Along the Way

Key moments that shaped our organization and mission.

2012

Founded in New Delhi

Started with a single education program serving 50 children in rural Rajasthan.

2014

First Clean Water Well

Built our first well in Makueni County, Kenya, providing water to 300 villagers.

2016

Healthcare Program Launched

Deployed first mobile clinic in Bangladesh, treating 500+ patients in month one.

2018

10,000 Children Educated

Reached the milestone of 10,000 children enrolled in education programs globally.

2020

COVID-19 Emergency Response

Distributed 500,000 relief kits and PPE to vulnerable communities during the pandemic.

2022

50 Countries Milestone

Expanded operations to 50+ countries, establishing a presence on every continent.

2025

2 Million Lives Impacted

Surpassed the incredible milestone of positively impacting 2 million lives worldwide.
